# Public roadmap

Updated: 24 November 2026. Priorities describe direction rather than delivery-date commitments.

| Sequence | Focus | Acceptance intent |
|---|---|---|
| Next | Durable shared-agent runs and versioned outputs | Progress, cancellation, restart recovery and evidence lineage |
| Next | Discovery → Requirements Readiness | Evidence-linked assessments and gaps that people can review |
| Before customer pilot | Permissions across derived outputs and actions | Validate access boundaries using real identities and restricted sources |
| Following | Planning Advisor → Dependency & Risk | Explain tool-calculated scenarios and cross-team impacts |
| Following | Delivery Intelligence | Ground narratives in measured execution history and disclose uncertainty |
| Later | Meeting & Decision and approved notifications | Review transcript-derived decisions and proposed actions before distribution |
| Optional extension | Rovo interoperability | Validate supported interfaces, permissions and customer value |
| Release gate | Enterprise qualification | Model evaluation, live integrations, isolation, restore, load and upgrade acceptance |

## Intended specialist responsibilities

- **Product Discovery:** evidence-linked journeys and requirement gaps.
- **Requirements Readiness:** readiness assessments and proposed questions.
- **Planning Advisor:** scenario explanations and capacity trade-offs.
- **Dependency & Risk:** dependency impacts and proposed mitigations.
- **Delivery Intelligence:** execution narratives grounded in calculated metrics.
- **Meeting & Decision:** draft decision logs and action proposals.

The six-agent suite is a target architecture. Existing discovery and planning capabilities do not imply that all six specialists are implemented or collaborating today.

## How progress will be communicated

Publish meaningful milestone notes when work and validation are complete. Separate implementation from deployment and acceptance. Avoid artificial commit activity, unsupported performance claims and customer data in public updates.
